Support

Loot Wars for iPhone

Found a bug, stuck on something, or have an idea? Email mistydual@gmail.com and we'll get back to you.

For a bug, it helps to include:

  • Your iPhone model and iOS version (Settings > General > About)
  • The game's version (Settings > About in the game)
  • What you were doing when it happened, and a screenshot if you can

How do I play?

Open How to Play from the title screen for a quick tour. In short: grab loot, wall in your base so it earns points, and use bombs to break into other bases and take their loot. The highest score when the clock runs out wins.

Is my progress saved?

Yes, on your iPhone. Your level, XP, best score and settings are saved on the device. There are no accounts, so deleting the game deletes your progress, and it doesn't move to a new phone.

Does it need the internet?

No. Every match is against bots and plays fully offline.

Are there ads or purchases?

No ads and no in-app purchases. Tickets and items are earned in the game and have no real-world value.

The game is too easy or too hard

Change the difficulty in Settings. Easy gives you the most help; Hardcore gives you none.

Which devices are supported?

iPhone running iOS 15.6 or later.
